Privacy Policy – Kachin Link

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We also store the posts, comments, messages and stories you create in Kachin Link.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app: to log you in, show your profile, display your posts and let you chat with other users.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Your posts are shown according to the visibility you choose (Only me, Friends or Public). Your data is stored using Google Firebase.

4. Data deletion

You can delete your posts at any time inside the app. To delete your account and all of your data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.
